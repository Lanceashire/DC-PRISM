# Consolidated research work, 7 November 2026

The previous public head (`a35fc466...`) contained supplement v1. This publication integrates the local Claude checkout through v11 (`f8a71bb` plus two uncommitted generator edits), the Codex continuation and bilingual deliverables, the Codex review snapshot, and the later Claude manuscript. The original source identities and version boundaries are retained.

The project began with risk-budgeted acquisition under delayed selective feedback. Audits repaired premature use of exploration labels and flaws in fixed-count/fixed-epsilon comparisons. Repair reruns, fresh confirmation and diagnostic simulations are kept distinct. Supplements v2–v4 examine common-family opportunity, additional baselines, natural replay and proxy/parameter diagnosis. V5 expands candidate families and fresh replay; its H study has 36,864 saved trajectories and 368,640 round rows, including the recovered compressed trajectory file. V6–v7 investigate probability-model recovery, outcome checks, downstream learners and analytic approximations. V8–v10 study greedy-oracle accounting, eight-cohort noise ordering, the MLP learner and risk-budget sensitivity.

V11 corrects and challenges the central claims: distribution-free empirical inequalities in 624 states; exact enumeration on 12-candidate pools; probability sharpness/shift sensitivity; recorded-outcome equivalence checks; within-cohort randomized label flips; pools of 24/48/96; and exact cardinality certification for the 88 previously uncertified references. The A/B/C1/C2/D manifest records 1,096 tasks and 4,728 rows, COMPLETE. These are saved outputs, not a fresh full rerun at publication time.

Codex checked v11 source hashes and empirical inequalities and revised the two language versions. Important qualifications concern the classical value-of-perfect-information interpretation, a former invalid independent-normal upper bound, expectation versus sample brackets, probability-model substitution, and optimistic post-hoc maxima of noisy policy estimates. The latest Claude manuscript is later than that review and restores stronger headline language in some places; both branches are preserved with explicit review status.

The defensible contribution is an audit of what an acquisition oracle may observe and what its lead measures. For a fixed family and information set, post-outcome perfect-information value differs from ex-ante strategy value. Greedy residuals also contain optimization and Monte Carlo effects. DC-PRISM has heterogeneous replay performance: gains over quantity references coexist with losses to information-greedy rules. A universally superior policy or fully identified real-world irreducible gap is not established.

All unique saved research results are included; 91 byte-identical runtime copies are mapped to their canonical files. Original third-party inputs and software environments are outside the publication. The interrupted Codex nested diagnostic contains 38 of 128 planned states and remains a separate incomplete archive. File hashes, compressed-row checks, source checks and publication provenance accompany the snapshot. Full experimental reproduction and submission acceptance remain separate tasks.

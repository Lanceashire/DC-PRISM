# Scientific review status / 当前学术审查状态

This note applies to the consolidated publication. It is not an acceptance certificate. Latest text and previously reviewed text are both archived; later modification does not imply later validation.

1. **Estimands / 估计目标。** `F = E max d - max E d` is fixed-family perfect-information value. `G_hold - G_cf` is a greedy diagnostic residual that may also contain search and simulation error. Statements interpreting a residual percentage as an identified irreducible floor require additional alignment and error control.
2. **Novelty / 新颖性。** EVPI is classical. The paper's potential contribution is its label-acquisition measurement protocol and empirical consequences. Algebraic nonnegativity and direct bounds alone are limited theoretical novelty; checking 624 sample inequalities is implementation validation.
3. **Probability substitution / 概率替代。** An independently fitted outcome model does not identify the real conditional law. Calibration of one hindsight functional and confidence intervals containing zero do not establish floor equivalence. Perturbation and TOST results must be reported at their actual aggregation level.
4. **Comparator / 比较器。** Cross-fitted greedy gain evaluates that finite-bank selector. It can fall below practical strategies because of weak search or simulation. Taking a per-state maximum over noisy held-out policy estimates introduces optimism. The two latest generator edits remove this maximum; best-fixed-strategy summaries still use the same study to choose the best fixed strategy and should be interpreted descriptively.
5. **Search / 搜索。** Exact checks cover 12-candidate pools. Small aggregate exact-minus-greedy differences do not certify larger pools or all individual cases.
6. **Noise and budget / 噪声与预算。** Cross-cohort correlations are descriptive. Random flips test a specified corruption intervention. The difference of two increasing opportunities need not increase with budget in general.
7. **Policy / 算法。** Mixed DC-PRISM results do not support universal superiority or isolate every component. The risk-envelope probability guarantee additionally needs conditional independence and valid upper scores; empirical risk scores alone do not ensure this.
8. **Latest-draft consistency / 最新稿一致性。** The later Claude abstract and framework use stronger “floor share” / luck-removal descriptions than the main-text qualifications justify. The older Codex-reviewed snapshot also retains some table-caption/wording inconsistencies identified in chat. Neither version is labelled submission-ready. Read numerical results under their original estimators.
9. **Review scope / 审查范围。** Source/hash and stored-output checks were performed, not a complete raw-data rerun or a complete independent audit of every citation and protocol decision. The manuscript's broad pre-registration language should be checked study by study against the saved freeze and history.

Priority work: align a single main estimand and family; independently select and evaluate a stronger ex-ante comparator; demonstrate that the protocol changes one concrete benchmark conclusion under defensible probability sensitivity. The snapshot supports further research and review, with these limitations visible.
